A patient teacher that talks, in your language.

The use case

Every student gets a tutor who never runs out of time.

A class of 40 has one teacher. A student who is stuck at 9 pm on a chemistry equation, or who learns best in Telugu or Tamil, often has nobody to ask. AIVidya gives them a tutor they can talk to, while keeping teachers and parents in the loop.

“Explain why the balanced equation for KMnO₄ + HCl needs those numbers, in Hindi, and then quiz me.”

Instead of searching through videos and guides, a student picks a topic, listens to a structured lesson, asks doubts by voice, text or a photo of their homework, and then checks what they have actually understood.

Instead of guessing who is falling behind, a teacher sees mastery heat-maps by chapter, reviews and corrects what the AI wrote, and assigns practice.

Instead of sending children's data to a cloud service, a school or family can run the whole thing on one machine.

Learn

Listen to a lesson read sentence by sentence with the words highlighted, or read the transcript. Switch to Quick mode or a one-page cheat sheet when time is short.

Ask

Ask doubts by voice, text or a photo. The tutor stays on topic, shows formulas, graphs and diagrams on a board, and cites textbook passages you upload.

Practise & revise

Adaptive quizzes, step-checked practice problems, flashcards on a schedule, timed tests and CBSE-pattern papers, plus a Daily 5 to keep a streak.

Who it's for

Four kinds of people, one app.

Students

Learn at your own pace

Browse topics, listen, ask, quiz yourself, take timed tests, revise on a schedule and earn XP. Add any topic you are curious about and get a private lesson.

Parents

Stay informed

Approve your child's account and see weekly progress and per-subject reports. Parents see progress, not chat transcripts.

Teachers

Teach with the AI, not behind it

Run classes, assign lessons and practice, answer the doubt inbox, make printable worksheets with answer keys, and approve, edit or reject AI lessons.

Admins

Run the school

Manage people, schools, curriculum and every lesson, send announcements, and keep an eye on backups and system health from a dedicated console.

How a lesson works

Twelve parts, from “why does this matter?” to “can I use it?”.

Each lesson is written one section at a time and cached, which keeps small local models reliable. Facts are automatically checked and the whole lesson is labelled as AI-written until a teacher reviews it.

Features

What's inside.

Voice tutor

Streaming answers read aloud, hands-free mode, barge-in, and private speech recognition with Whisper plus Piper voices on your own server.

Learner model

Bayesian knowledge tracing with a forgetting curve decides what to revise next, with a daily plan and exam countdown.

Tools for science & maths

12 simulations, periodic table, equation balancer, 3D molecules, SymPy algebra, unit conversion and a sandboxed Python/HTML runner.

Textbook grounding

Upload your licensed textbooks. Lessons and chat cite the passages they used. No copyrighted text is bundled.

Safe by design

Parental consent under 18, distress detection with help-line numbers, rate limits, strict CSP, a no-network code sandbox, and data export and erasure.

Accessible

Light, dark and high-contrast themes, text size, dyslexia-friendly font and line spacing, with axe-core checks on key pages.

20 languages, one button.

Choose a language in the top-right corner and the interface, the lessons and answers, and the voice all change together.

Please read before putting this in front of children

The AI still makes mistakes, including wrong reasoning and weaker lessons in the less common languages. Treat every lesson as a draft until a teacher has reviewed it. Real speakers, microphones and phones, the Claude API path and e-mail/push delivery have not been verified end to end, and the privacy notice and terms are templates for a lawyer to finish.

Quick start

Run it on your own machine.

You need Python and Ollama. A GPU helps; on an RTX 4090 a lesson starts showing in about 10 seconds.

git clone https://github.com/sadiqgpasha89/Ai-Vidya-Tutor.git
cd Ai-Vidya-Tutor

./install.sh              # Python env, offline libraries, Ollama models, first admin
./scripts/pull_models.sh  # gemma4:e4b, gpt-oss:20b, translategemma:4b, nomic-embed-text
./run.sh                  # open http://127.0.0.1:8000

Just trying it? TEACHER_MOCK=1 ./run.sh serves sample content with no model, and TEACHER_AUTH=off ./run.sh skips sign-in. Prefer containers? docker compose up -d --build (see the deployment guide). Use Chrome or Edge for browser voice input.
