@subject physics
@grade 11
+ Advanced Classical Mechanics (without vectors or calculus) | Non‑inertial reference frames and fictitious forces (centrifugal, Coriolis); Energy methods for simple pendulum – small‑amplitude vs large‑amplitude; Effective potential in central‑force problems (e.g., planetary motion, Kepler’s laws); Rotational dynamics of rigid bodies – moment of inertia for common shapes, parallel‑axis theorem; Conservation of angular momentum in planar collisions and explosions
+ Elementary Statistical Mechanics & Kinetic Theory | Mean free path, collision cross‑section and pressure derivation from kinetic theory; Maxwell–Boltzmann speed distribution – mean, rms, most probable speeds; Equipartition theorem for translational, rotational, and vibrational degrees of freedom; Ideal gas law from kinetic theory – derivation of PV = NkT; Entropy as a measure of disorder – combinatorial definition and simple applications
+ Fluid Mechanics & Wave Phenomena (purely algebraic) | Continuity equation for incompressible flow – derivation and applications (pipe flow, jet streams); Bernoulli’s principle – derivation from work–energy and applications to lift, Venturi effect; Sound waves in fluids – speed of sound, reflection, refraction, standing waves in pipes; Resonance in mechanical and acoustic systems – quality factor, damping, beats; Surface tension and capillary rise – Laplace pressure, Jurin’s law
@grade 12
+ Advanced Electromagnetic Phenomena | Magnetic Flux and Its Quantification; Faraday’s Law – Induced EMF in Moving Conductors; Lenz’s Law and Energy Conservation in Induction; Self‑Inductance of Solenoids and Toroids; Resonant Circuits – Series and Parallel RLC; Electromagnetic Energy Storage and Power Losses
+ Foundations of Quantum Mechanics for Olympiad | Wave‑Particle Duality – de Broglie Relation; Time‑Independent Schrödinger Equation (1‑D); Quantisation in Infinite and Finite Potential Wells; Hydrogen Atom – Energy Levels and Radial Functions; Selection Rules for Electric Dipole Transitions; Spin, Pauli Exclusion, and Basic Spectroscopy
+ Advanced Wave Optics and Fourier Analysis | Young’s Double‑Slit and Multi‑Slit Interference; Fraunhofer Diffraction – Single, Double, and Grating; Polarisation – Linear, Circular, and Elliptical; Wavefronts, Huygens’ Principle, and Lens Aberrations; Fourier Transform of Aperture Functions (Fraunhofer); Resolution Limits – Rayleigh Criterion and Diffraction‑Limited Imaging
