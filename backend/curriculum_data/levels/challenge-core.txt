# Hand-written Challenge extensions for Class 9-10 science (the AI drafts for these classes went beyond what students can follow).
# Challenge level only. Same format as the board overlays; edit freely.
@subject physics
@grade 9
+ Motion Graphs and Problem Solving | Area under a velocity-time graph; Relative motion in a straight line; Free fall problems; Average speed versus average velocity
+ Equilibrium and Moments | Moment of a force; Centre of mass; Stable and unstable equilibrium; Density and Archimedes' principle problems
@grade 10
+ Optics Problem Solving | Sign convention for mirrors and lenses; Lenses in contact; Refraction through a prism; Total internal reflection and its uses
+ Circuit Problem Solving | Series-parallel networks; Current and voltage division; Electrical power and energy bills; Fuses and household wiring
@subject chemistry
@grade 9
+ Mole Concept Practice | Mole and Avogadro's number; Percentage composition; Empirical formula; Mass and volume relationships
+ Separation Techniques in Depth | Chromatography and Rf values; Fractional distillation; Crystallisation; Testing purity by melting and boiling points
@grade 10
+ Stoichiometry Problems | Balancing by inspection and by oxidation number; Limiting reagent; Percentage yield; Gas volumes at STP
+ Organic Reasoning | Naming simple carbon compounds; Isomerism basics; Trends in a homologous series; Tests for functional groups
@subject biology
@grade 9
+ Cell Biology in Depth | Microscopy and magnification calculations; Osmosis experiments; Organelles and what goes wrong in disease; Stages of cell division
@grade 10
+ Genetics Problem Solving | Monohybrid crosses and Punnett squares; Test cross; Sex determination; Reading Mendel's data
